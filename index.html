Our Story

FAH was created with a simple idea: bring the joy of familiar Indian flavours into a modern snacking experience.

We believe that some of the best food memories come from simple, traditional snacks shared with family and friends.

FAH takes that familiar spirit and presents it in a contemporary, premium format designed for today's customers.

Our first product, FAH Crunchies, brings a bold Desi Masala flavour and a satisfying crunch together in a convenient pack.

FAH — Find All Happiness represents the small moments of happiness that can come from enjoying good food, sharing a snack and taking a break from the everyday.

Our vision is to build FAH into a brand that celebrates Indian snack culture while creating products that fit naturally into modern life.
